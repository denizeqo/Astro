🌙 {% trans "The 28 Lunar Mansions" %}

{% trans "The" %} {% trans "28 Lunar Mansions," %} {% trans "also called lunar stations, represent an ancient division of the Moon's path through the sky. Each mansion is symbolically connected to a" %} {% trans "star or constellation" %} {% trans "and represents a particular energy or celestial spirit." %}

{% trans "In ancient" %} {% trans "Arabic, Indian and Chinese astrological traditions," %} {% trans "the lunar cycle played a fundamental role in observing and understanding the rhythms of life. The lunar Zodiac was therefore considered a more intimate and profound dimension than the solar Zodiac." %}

{% trans "The 28 mansions are distributed along the zodiacal path, symbolically beginning at 0° Aries and overlapping the twelve zodiac signs. They are also divided into masculine and feminine mansions. This distinction depends on the relationship between opposite mansions and their rising and setting in relation to the horizon." %}
