{% blocktrans %} The seven planetary spheres were arranged as follows:
7th Sphere — Saturn Discipline, limitation, time, and structure.
6th Sphere — Jupiter Expansion, wisdom, growth, and knowledge.
5th Sphere — Mars Action, strength, courage, and will.
4th Sphere — Sun Center, light, identity, and vitality.
3rd Sphere — Venus Love, harmony, beauty, and relationship.
2nd Sphere — Mercury Thought, communication, and knowledge.
1st Sphere — Moon Emotions, memory, sensitivity, and inner life. The Astro Magnetism Vision {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} For Astro Magnetism, this ancient sequence becomes a symbolic map of energy: from the spheres closest to Earth to the Stars and Constellations, each level represents a different force that participates in the interpretation of the Astrological Chart. The Stars indicate the ancestral energy; the Planets show how this energy manifests and moves through life. {% endblocktrans %}
