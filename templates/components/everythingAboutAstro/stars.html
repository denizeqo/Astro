{% trans "⭐ The Fixed Stars" %}

{% trans "The Star is a Source of Light and Has Always Guided Humanity" %}

{% blocktrans %} In the sky, stars form 88 ancient and modern constellations, creating a great symbolic map of the cosmos. Astrology observes Stars, Planets and Houses. Stars represent the fixed and ancestral dimension of the sky, while Planets express the movement of energies. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}The Constellations ; In ancient traditions, the sky was interpreted through three great worlds:
Heaven — the constellations of the upper world, associated with air and winged beings: Cygnus, Aquila, Pegasus, etc.
Earth — the constellations associated with the terrestrial and human world: Perseus, Virgo, Serpent, Orion, etc.
Water — the constellations associated with the lower world and the waters: Pisces, Cetus, Argo Navis, etc. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "This vision creates a great symbolic map of Sky, Earth, and Water, through which ancient humanity sought to understand the relationship between the visible and invisible worlds." %}

{% trans "The Star Code." %}

{% blocktrans %}In the Astro Magnetism method, each Star is studied through its energetic code: Position → Element → Symbol → Number The Position indicates where it acts. The Element indicates how it acts. The Symbol expresses its nature. The Number represents its code. The Essence For this reason, each Star can express a different energetic quality. The Fixed Stars therefore represent an ancestral and enduring energy that accompanies the Astrological Chart and is interpreted together with planetary energies. The Planet shows movement. The Star preserves memory. The Constellation indicates the field in which the energy manifests.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}For ancient traditions, stars were not simply points of light in the sky: they represented sacred and archetypal forces, connected with the gods, the soul, and destiny. For centuries, Astronomy and Astrology were deeply interconnected. Fixed Stars held a central place in the study of the sky and were considered carriers of particular qualities, gifts, and challenges. The ancients saw the sky as a manifestation of the divine.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "The Star and the Soul." %}

{% blocktrans %}According to this vision, being born under a particular Constellation creates a symbolic correspondence between the soul and the sky. Aries, Taurus, Gemini, and the other Constellations thus become archetypes through which qualities and paths of existence can be interpreted. The Stars are called “fixed” not because they are motionless, but because their apparent movement is extremely slow compared with that of the Planets. For this reason, in the astrological tradition, they represent deep, ancient, and enduring energies.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Star and Planet." %}

{% blocktrans %}The Star is not interpreted in isolation. When a Planet enters into relationship with a Star, the two energies meet and may intensify one another. In the Astro Magnetism method, this encounter is defined as Consecration.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "The Star preserves the ancestral energy. The Planet activates it. Their union makes the energy manifest." %}

{% trans "The Essence." %}

{% trans "The Star represents what is ancient and permanent; the Planet represents what moves and activates." %}

{% trans "ASTRO MAGNETISM STUDY AND THE FIXED STARS" %}

{% trans "In the Astro Magnetism method, the Fixed Stars represent one of the fundamental foundations of Astrological Chart interpretation. Their influence is studied through several essential elements:" %}

{% trans "Magnitude." %}

{% trans "The magnitude indicates the apparent brightness of a Star. In the astrological tradition, the brightest Stars are considered particularly significant." %}

{% trans "Celestial Position." %}

{% trans "The Star’s position in the sky, especially its relationship with the Ecliptic, helps establish its connection with the Astrological Chart." %}

{% trans "Nature of the Star and the Planet." %}

{% trans "When the symbolic nature of the Star is compatible with that of the Planet, the two energies can create a stronger correspondence." %}

{% trans "Rising, Culmination, and Alignment." %}

{% blocktrans %}A Star assumes particular importance when it rises, culminates, or enters into relationship through an Alignment with a significant point or Planet in the Chart.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Aspects of the Fixed Stars." %}

{% blocktrans %}The position of the Star in relation to the Planets and the four cardinal points — Ascendant, Midheaven, Descendant, and Imum Coeli — makes it possible to identify the most important connections.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "The Stars in Your Astrological Chart." %}

{% blocktrans %}In the Astro Magnetism Chart, one of the first elements we observe is the Fixed Stars connected with your birth. Your birth date and time allow us to identify the Stars that, according to our method, form the energetic foundation of the Chart.
These energies are then studied in relation to:
Stars → Planets → Houses → Cardinal Points → Transits → Progressions
In this way, we do not observe only which Star is present, but also how and when its energy is activated.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "The Fundamental Principle." %}

{% blocktrans %}A Star may be important at birth, but it can take on renewed strength when it is activated by a Planet, a Cardinal Point, a Transit, or a Progression. For this reason, the study of the Stars is not limited to “what energy you have”, but also considers: when it is activated, how it manifests, and in which area of life it may express itself.{% endblocktrans %}
