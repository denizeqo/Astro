Astro Numerology
Wheel of Destiny

ASTRO-NUMEROLOGY represents a discipline of ancient origin, rooted in a thousand-year-old tradition dating back to about 5,000 years ago. This practice, which has crossed several civilizations including Chaldean, Egyptian, Latin and Greek, is universally recognized as the "Wheel of Destiny", based on the date of birth of each individual.
