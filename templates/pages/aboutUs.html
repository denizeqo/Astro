A Vision Born from a Lifetime

Astro Magnetism is an integrated system of self-knowledge that combines Astrology, Astro-Numerology and Astro Magnetism, creating a personal and profound vision of the human being. This platform is born from the study of the relationship between Macrocosm and Microcosm and of the laws that govern the Universe and life, connecting what exists in the greater Universe with what lives and manifests within the human being.

At the foundation of this vision is a fundamental principle: every human being is shaped by a law of birth, experience and transformation.

Throughout life, the human being exists between two great dimensions: above and below, Spirit and Soul. Spirit represents the unconscious, the higher dimension of our being: our inner potential, the unconscious, ancestral energies, archetypes, innate vocations and natural talents that we carry within us. Soul represents the conscious and tangible dimension: our experience, our choices, our emotions and the life we live on Earth.

The journey of life consists of creating a conscious balance between these two dimensions, so that the potential of Spirit can find expression in reality through the Soul. For this reason, our task is not to place one force against the other, but to harmonize Spirit and Soul within the different spheres of life. When these two dimensions come into harmony, the potential we carry within us can begin to fully manifest in our lives.
