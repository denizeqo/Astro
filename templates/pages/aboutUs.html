I proudly present this unique study, born from a deep passion and vocation, embodying research capable of reflecting the experience of an entire lifetime. This work establishes an unprecedented approach, integrating astrology and numerology with absolute originality. We are speaking of an ancient discipline, rooted in tradition, yet distinguished by being simultaneously innovative and scientific; it was also defined as “the meaning of life.”

Astro-numerology is not merely a curiosity, but a discipline with extremely ancient origins, whose validity is confirmed by a millennial tradition. This practice, while preserving its mysterious and transcendental character, has crossed and influenced various civilizations, including the Chaldean, Egyptian, Latin, and Greek cultures. Among the Egyptians, it was officially recognized as the “Wheel of Destiny” and the healing through sleep. In esotericism, this knowledge was reserved exclusively for disciples or initiates, while in magnetism it found space especially within the French tradition, where it became known as mesmerism. Mozart, by portraying in the opera Così fan tutte a parody of magnetic therapy (with reference to the so-called septenary), clearly demonstrates how all these ancient traditions and disciplines were founded upon the influence of the stars. It becomes evident that their energy or fluid upon the functions of the human body (or any living being) possesses its own “magnetic” nature in correspondence with the celestial bodies; consider, for example, how the Moon causes the tides, implying that blood itself is attributed divine roles and immense powers.

The people of Ancient Egypt built their entire philosophy upon two parallel and inseparable lives:

    The eternal and immortal life belonging to the spirit.
    The earthly life connected to the soul (understood as the earthly spirit), which is bound to follow the spirit.

Through matter, namely our physical body, the soul must necessarily undertake a path of progress toward wisdom and enlightenment already written within your destiny.
