What Is Astrology?

Astrology is an ancient tradition based on the observation of the sky and celestial bodies. Throughout the centuries, humanity has observed the movement of Stars, Planets, and Constellations, seeking to understand their relationship with life on Earth.

Astrology is not currently considered an official science by the scientific community. It is a traditional and symbolic discipline that has developed through centuries of observation and experience. Its language uses many elements also found in astronomy: Stars, Planets, Constellations, Houses, coordinates, and celestial cycles.

In the astrological tradition, these elements are used to interpret personality, characteristics, potential, and cycles of life, beginning with the date and time of birth and subsequently observing Transits and Progressions.

Throughout history, Astrology has also intersected with other traditional forms of knowledge, such as Numerology, Geometry, and Alchemy, creating an increasingly rich symbolic language.

In Summary astrology observes the sky in search of meaning in life on Earth.

The Zodiac and Precession

To understand Astrology, it is important to know a fundamental astronomical phenomenon: the Precession of the Equinoxes. Earth's axis slowly performs a circular movement through space. This produces a very slow displacement of the equinoctial point along the Zodiac. A complete precession cycle lasts about 26,000 years.

The point we call 0° Aries therefore does not remain fixed relative to the stars. Over millennia it slowly moves through the constellations. The discovery of precession is traditionally attributed to Hipparchus, around the 2nd century BCE.

This creates an important distinction between zodiac signs and constellations. The sign of Aries, for example, no longer exactly coincides with the constellation Aries. Today the equinoctial point associated with the beginning of Aries lies in the region of Pisces.

From this comes the concept of Astrological Ages, determined by the slow movement of the equinoctial point through the constellations. According to one of the most widespread traditional divisions, we are in the concluding phase of the Age of Pisces and approaching the Age of Aquarius. The exact date is not universal because it depends on how constellation boundaries are defined.

Alrisha — The Cord That Unites the Pisces.

The constellation of Pisces carries a particularly meaningful symbolism: two fish connected by a cord. At the point where they meet is Alrisha, a star that, in tradition, represents the bond between the two Fish.

Symbolically, the two fish move in different directions: one toward the sky, the other toward the horizon. In astrological and symbolic tradition, this image represents a duality: spirit and matter, heaven and earth, inner evolution and practical life.

The cord therefore becomes a powerful symbol: that which unites two forces that appear to be opposite.

🌙 The 28 Lunar Mansions

The 28 Lunar Mansions, also called lunar stations, represent an ancient division of the Moon's path through the sky. Each mansion is symbolically connected to a star or constellation and represents a particular energy or celestial spirit.

In ancient Arabic, Indian and Chinese astrological traditions, the lunar cycle played a fundamental role in observing and understanding the rhythms of life. The lunar Zodiac was therefore considered a more intimate and profound dimension than the solar Zodiac.

The 28 mansions are distributed along the zodiacal path, symbolically beginning at 0° Aries and overlapping the twelve zodiac signs. They are also divided into masculine and feminine mansions. This distinction depends on the relationship between opposite mansions and their rising and setting in relation to the horizon.

Astro-Numerology — The Science of Numbers

Astro-Numerology studies the symbolic power of numbers and their correspondences with the order of the Universe. In ancient tradition, a number was not considered merely a quantity, but a force, a form, and a principle capable of connecting the spiritual, astral, and material worlds.

1

Unity & Progression

All numbers symbolically derive from Unity. Numbers 1–10 represent fundamental principles of the spiritual plane and the progression through which energy manifests.

⚖

Male & Female Polarity

Odd: masculine and active — 1, 3, 5, 7, 9.
Even: feminine and receptive — 2, 4, 6, 8, 10. These are complementary polarities, not good and evil.

3

Three Planes of Existence

1 — Spiritual: inner and divine. 2 — Astral: vital and energetic. 3 — Material: bodily and concrete.

²

The Life of Numbers

Simple number → spiritual plane. Number squared → astral plane. Number cubed → material plane.

∑

The Essential Root

The essential root is obtained by progressively adding all numbers up to the number considered. Example: 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10, so 4 has essential root 10.

427

Composite Numbers

427 → 4 + 2 + 7 = 13 → 1 + 3 = 4. In symbolic reading, 427 therefore carries the essence of 4.

◀

The Head of the Number

In a composite number, the first digit on the left represents the head or dominant principle, indicating its family and main direction.

🪞

Symmetrical Numbers

Composite numbers can have a symmetrical number by reversing their digits: 13 ↔ 31, 24 ↔ 42. These are considered numbers in relationship. Identical digits such as 11, 22, 33, 44 and 55 are called “egoistic” numbers.

↗

Ascending & Descending

Numbers can be observed through ascending and descending progressions, showing how digits aggregate and transform according to their order.

0

The Meaning of Zero

Zero has a particular function. In the numerological tradition described, adding zero to an odd number can modify its polarity: 3 → 30, while an even number retains its nature: 4 → 40.

The Anatomy of the Number

Every number has its own structure, like an organism. Its anatomy allows us to understand how it is composed, which elements form it, which family it belongs to and which position it occupies in the series.

Spirit → the sum of the digits. Body → what remains after extracting the spirit. Astral → the level emerging from the relationship between mind and body.

Example: 823. 8 + 2 + 3 = 13 → Spirit. 823 − 13 = 810. 8 + 1 + 0 = 9. 810 − 9 = 801. In the model presented: 13 = Spirit, 801 = Astral, 9 = Body.

The Law of the Ternary

The constitution of the number comprises three principles: 1 — Active 2 — Passive 3 — Neutral, resulting from the interaction between the first two. The fundamental formula is: 1 + 2 = 3 The simplest example is the family: Father = 1 → Active Mother = 2 → Passive Child = 3 → Neutral Father + Mother = Child → Family The family thus becomes a Unit of a higher order, represented by 4. 1 + 2 + 3 = 4 This is the structure of the Quaternary: the Ternary contained within Unity.
